Questions about cost and scope

How much does a project cost?

Websites start from KES 25,000 for a one-page site and KES 60,000 for a full business site. Business systems start from KES 350,000. The price estimator gives you a range for your project, and you get a fixed price in a written proposal before any work begins.

How do payments work?

You pay by M-Pesa or bank transfer, split into stages that are written into the proposal. A typical split is 40% to start, 40% when you approve the working demo, and 20% at handover.

What if we need to change something halfway?

Small changes are part of the weekly review. If a change adds real work, we tell you the cost and the effect on the timeline before doing it, and you decide.

Who owns the code?

You do. Once the project is paid in full, the source code, accounts and documentation are transferred to you. You are free to take it to another developer.

Not sure which one you need?

Describe the problem in a few lines. We will tell you what we would do and roughly what it would cost.
