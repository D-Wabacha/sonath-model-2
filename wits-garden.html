School website

Wits Garden Educational Centre

A five-page website for a community school in Dandora Phase 5, Nairobi. It covers the classes, daily school life, enrolment enquiries and ways to support the school.

Location

Visit the live site

The job

Wits Garden teaches children from Play Group to Grade 8. The school needed one place where parents can see what is taught and ask about enrolment.

It also needed to speak to a second audience: people and organisations who want to support the school, and who need to know how their help would be used.

What we built

  • Five pages: Home, Classes and services, School life, Contact and Donate
  • A class-by-class overview from Play Group to Grade 8, including French and computer studies
  • A photo and video gallery of classrooms and daily school life
  • An enquiry form, click-to-call and WhatsApp links, and a map of the school
  • A Donate page explaining what support pays for, with a form to register interest
  • Answers to common questions about fees, hours and enrolment
