Hotel website

Royal Tavern Hotel

A website for a hotel in Othaya, Nyeri County. It shows the rooms, dining, gardens and conference spaces, and gets a guest to a phone call or WhatsApp message in one tap.

Visit the live site

The job

Royal Tavern hosts overnight guests, private dinners, garden events and conferences, and it takes its bookings by phone and WhatsApp.

The site had two jobs. Show everything the hotel offers on one page, and make the call or message button impossible to miss, on a phone as much as on a laptop.

What we built

  • One long page covering rooms, private dining, garden gatherings, the gazebo, conferences, location and contact
  • Call and WhatsApp links in the header, in the opening section and on a button that stays on screen while you scroll
  • A conferences and events section with its own enquiry buttons
  • A map of the hotel in Othaya with a link for directions
  • A contact panel with phone, WhatsApp, email, social accounts and opening hours
  • A layout that adapts from phone to desktop
