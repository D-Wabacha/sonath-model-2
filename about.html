Our story

Why Sonath exists

Most software is built for the widest possible audience, which means it rarely fits any one business particularly well. Established companies end up working around their tools instead of the other way around, patching together spreadsheets, plugins, and manual processes to cover the gaps.

Sonath was built to do the opposite: start with how a business actually operates, then build the system around it. That means fewer workarounds, less retraining, and software that keeps pace with the business rather than constraining it.

We work directly with the businesses we build for, from the first conversation through to handover and ongoing support, with no account managers or layers in between.
