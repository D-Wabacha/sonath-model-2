Why Sonath exists

Most software is made for the widest possible audience, so it rarely fits any one business well.

Companies end up working around their tools. The gaps get filled with spreadsheets, plugins and manual steps, and every new member of staff has to learn the workarounds.

We start from the other end. First we learn how the business runs, then we build the system around that. The result is fewer workarounds, less retraining, and software that keeps up as the business grows.

We are a team of two, and we mean to keep projects personal. The people you speak to on the first call are the people who design, build and support your project.

What we commit to

  • A fixed price in writing. You approve the scope and cost before we start.
  • An update every Friday. You never have to chase us to find out where things stand.
  • Full ownership. The code, the accounts and the documents are yours once the project is paid.
  • Honest advice. If an existing tool would do the job for less, we will tell you.
  • Confidentiality. We are happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before you share details.
