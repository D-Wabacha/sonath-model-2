What gets between a guest and a booking

Most lost bookings are not about price. They are about a guest who could not find an answer quickly enough.

  1. They cannot see the rooms

    A few photos on Facebook do not say what the room looks like, what it costs and what is included.

  2. The number is hard to find

    If calling or messaging takes more than one tap on a phone, many guests move on to the next place.

  3. Events go unasked

    Wedding and conference organisers need capacity, packages and a contact. Without them, they never call.

  4. Nobody can find the gate

    Directions, a map pin and a Google listing matter as much as the website itself.

Questions hotels ask

Can guests pay a deposit on the site?

Yes. M-Pesa deposits are an add-on to either package. The guest pays from their phone and you see the payment against the booking.

Can it connect to Booking.com or Airbnb?

We can link to your listings from the site. Keeping room availability in step across those platforms needs a separate tool called a channel manager. We can help you choose one as part of a technical review.

Can we change rates and photos ourselves?

Yes, if you want to. We can add a simple editing page for rates, menus and photos, or make the changes for you under a care plan.

What do you need from us?

Good photos of the rooms and grounds, your rates, menus and event packages, and one person who can answer questions each week. If you do not have good photos yet, we will tell you which ones matter most.

Tell us about your property.

How many rooms, how guests book today, and what you want to change. We reply within one working day.
