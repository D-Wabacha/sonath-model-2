How a project runs

Five stages, in the same order every time. At each one you receive something in writing, so you always know where things stand.

  1. Discovery

    Week 1

    We learn how the work gets done today and where it goes wrong.

  2. Proposal

    Week 1 to 2

    You get the scope, timeline and a fixed price in writing.

  3. Build

    2 to 10 weeks

    A progress update and a version to try every Friday.

  4. Handover

    Final week

    Launch, staff training, and all code and logins passed to you.

  5. Support

    Ongoing

    Free fixes for the first 30 days, then an optional care plan.

Common questions

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

How much does a project cost?

Websites start from KES 25,000 for a one-page site and KES 60,000 for a full business site. Business systems start from KES 350,000. Try the price estimator for your project. You get a fixed price in a written proposal before any work begins.

How long does it take?

Most websites take 2 to 4 weeks. Business systems take 6 to 12 weeks, depending on how much there is to build. The proposal includes a week-by-week timeline.

Who owns the code?

You do. Once the project is paid in full, the source code, accounts and documentation are transferred to you. You are free to take it to another developer.

What do you need from us?

One person who can answer questions and approve work each week. We also need your content: text, photos, price lists and any spreadsheets you use today.

What happens after launch?

We fix any faults for 30 days at no charge. After that you can take a monthly care plan, or run things yourself with the documentation we hand over.

Have a project in mind?

Tell us what you need. We reply within one working day with questions or a time to talk.
