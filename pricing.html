    Questions about pricing

    Something else? Ask us directly.

    Why a range and not one price?

    Two projects with the same ticks can still differ: how many photos need editing, how many room types, how messy the old spreadsheets are. The short call answers those questions, and then you get one fixed price in writing.

    How do I pay?

    By M-Pesa or bank transfer, in stages written into the proposal. A typical split is 40% to start, 40% when you approve the working demo, and 20% at handover.

    Are the domain and hosting included?

    We set them up for you, in your name, so you own them. The yearly fees are paid to the providers directly, and are usually a few thousand shillings a year for a small site. A care plan can manage the renewals for you.

    Will the price change halfway?

    Not without your say. If you ask for something that adds real work, we tell you the cost and the effect on the timeline before doing it, and you decide.

    Can we start small and add more later?

    Yes. You can start with a Launch page or Business site and add bookings, a shop or a system later. We build so that the next step does not mean starting again.

    Prefer to talk it through?

    Tell us what the business does and what is not working. We will say which package fits, or whether you need one at all.
