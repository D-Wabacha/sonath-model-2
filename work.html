How we work

A fixed process, not a black box.

Every engagement follows the same five stages, so you always know what happens next.

01 Discovery

We start by understanding how the business actually runs today: the systems already in place, the workarounds people rely on, and where things break down. This shapes everything that follows.

02 Scoping

A clear, fixed-scope proposal setting out what will be built, in what order, and what it will cost. No vague estimates and no scope that quietly expands once work begins.

03 Build

Development happens in focused stages with regular check-ins along the way, so there are no surprises when it's time to review progress.

04 Handover

Documentation, training, and a clean transition to your team, with nothing left undocumented or quietly dependent on us to keep running.

05 Support

Ongoing support after launch, so the system keeps working as the business around it continues to change.
