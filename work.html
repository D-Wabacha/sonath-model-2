Example build

Orders and stock for a hardware supplier

The two projects above are websites. To show what a business system looks like, here is an example we drew: one screen for the office, and a simple order form for sales staff on their phones.

What a system like this covers

  • Orders entered once, on phone or desktop
  • Stock that updates with every sale
  • Low-stock warnings before you run out
  • Invoices and unpaid balances per customer
  • Staff logins with different permissions
  • Weekly and monthly reports
