Process

Five stages, and something in writing at each one.

Every project follows the same order. You always know what is happening now, what comes next, and what it costs.

Stage 1Week 1

Discovery

A call or a visit to understand how the work gets done today, who does it, and where it goes wrong. We ask to see the spreadsheets, forms and messages you rely on.

You receive

  • A one-page summary of what we heard
  • Our view of what needs building, and what does not

What we ask in discovery

  1. What does a normal day look like for the people who will use this?
  2. Where do things get lost, delayed or entered twice?
  3. Which tools do you use now, and what do you like about them?
  4. Who needs to see what, and who should not?
  5. What would make this project a success a year from now?

Stage 2Week 1 to 2

Proposal

A written proposal that lists what will be built, in what order, how long it will take and what it will cost. The price is fixed. Nothing starts until you approve it.

You receive

  • The list of features, in plain language
  • A week-by-week timeline
  • A fixed price and payment stages

Stage 32 to 10 weeks

Build

We build in weekly steps. Every Friday you get a short written update and a private link where you can try the latest version yourself. If something looks wrong, you see it that week.

You receive

  • A written update every Friday
  • A private test link that always shows the latest version
  • One clear list of anything we need from you

Stage 4Final week

Handover

We launch, train your team, and pass everything over: the code, the accounts and the guides. Nothing is left that only we know how to run.

You receive

  • The source code and all logins
  • Hosting and domain in your name
  • A user guide and a recorded training session

Stage 5Ongoing

Support

If anything we built does not work as agreed, we fix it free of charge for the first 30 days. After that you can take a monthly care plan, or look after it yourself with the guides we hand over.

You receive

  • A direct line to the two people who built it
  • Fixes for faults at no charge in the first 30 days

Questions about working together

What do you need from us?

One person who can answer questions and approve work each week. We also need your content: text, photos, price lists and any spreadsheets you use today.

What if we need to change something halfway?

Small changes are part of the weekly review. If a change adds real work, we tell you the cost and the effect on the timeline before doing it, and you decide.

How long does it take?

Most websites take 2 to 4 weeks. Business systems take 6 to 12 weeks, depending on how much there is to build. The proposal includes a week-by-week timeline.

What happens after launch?

We fix any faults for 30 days at no charge. After that you can take a monthly care plan, or run things yourself with the documentation we hand over.

Ready for stage one?
