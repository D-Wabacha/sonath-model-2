Demo sites

Four businesses that don't exist. Four websites that do.

We built complete websites for four imagined Kenyan businesses: a school, a property agency, a clinic and a boutique hotel. Open the one closest to your business and click around before you spend a shilling.

Each demo says so on the site itself. Names, prices and contact details are made up, photos are stock images, and nothing you type into a demo form is sent.

Demo 1 of 4 · School

Cedarfield Academy

A school website that takes a family from first look to admissions enquiry.

A fictional day school in Nairobi for children aged 4 to 14, from PP1 to Grade 9. Seven pages built around what parents ask: the stages, how admissions work, what the school offers after lessons, and how to get in touch.

  • Private schools
  • Academies
  • Learning centres
  • Day nurseries
Open the demo Estimate a site like this

What it shows

  • Four learning stages, PP1 to Grade 9, each with its own programme
  • A five-step admissions guide with key dates, documents and FAQs
  • A downloadable admissions guide (PDF)
  • Clubs and activities that parents can filter by interest
  • News and events, each with its own page
  • Quick and full enquiry forms that ask only for a parent’s details
  • Call, WhatsApp and Enquire buttons fixed to the bottom of the phone screen

Try this in one minute

  1. Open Admissions and read the five steps a family goes through.
  2. On School Life, filter the clubs by STEM.
  3. Try the quick admissions enquiry on the home page. It checks your entries and never asks for the child’s details.

Demo 2 of 4 · Property

Havenstone Realty

A property website where people search, shortlist and book a viewing themselves.

A fictional Nairobi agency with 17 listings to rent and buy across 14 neighbourhoods. Visitors filter by budget and bedrooms, save a shortlist, and request a viewing at a time that suits them.

  • Estate agents
  • Letting agents
  • Property managers
  • Developers
Open the demo Estimate a site like this

What it shows

  • Search by area, with filters for rent or buy, type, bedrooms and budget
  • Sorting by price or newest
  • A shortlist of saved homes, with a counter in the header
  • A page per listing with photos, key details, service charge and amenities
  • Viewing requests with a preferred day and time
  • A WhatsApp enquiry button on every listing
  • Separate desks for lettings, sales and commercial space

Try this in one minute

  1. Pick a neighbourhood under Search by area, such as Lavington.
  2. Open All properties, choose Buy and 4+ bedrooms, then sort by price.
  3. Tap the heart on a listing, then open Saved to see your shortlist.

Demo 3 of 4 · Clinic

WillowCare Clinics

A clinic website that is calm, clear, and careful with patient privacy.

A fictional outpatient practice with medical, dental and diagnostic services. It shows whether the clinic is open right now, explains each service, and takes appointment enquiries without asking for any health details.

  • Clinics
  • Dental practices
  • Medical centres
  • Pharmacies
Open the demo Estimate a site like this

What it shows

  • Shows whether the clinic is open now, in Nairobi time
  • A service directory with a page for each service
  • Appointment enquiries by form, phone or WhatsApp
  • Forms that never ask for symptoms or medical history
  • A team page laid out for registration numbers
  • Patient information and common questions
  • Emergency numbers (999 and 112) at the top of every page

Try this in one minute

  1. Look at the top of the home page: it tells you whether the clinic is open right now.
  2. Open Services and filter by Dental or Diagnostics.
  3. Start an appointment enquiry and notice what it does not ask you.

Demo 4 of 4 · Hotel

Amani House

A hotel website that feels like the stay, and makes the booking enquiry easy.

A fictional boutique retreat with seven rooms and suites. Guests choose dates on the first screen, compare rooms, and send an enquiry by form or a WhatsApp message that already names the room.

  • Boutique hotels
  • Lodges and camps
  • Guesthouses
  • Serviced apartments
Open the demo Estimate a site like this

What it shows

  • Dates and guests on the first screen
  • Seven rooms and suites, filtered by type and party size
  • A page per room with size, bed, view, amenities and rate
  • Dining, experiences and a photo gallery
  • A booking enquiry form that works out the length of stay
  • WhatsApp enquiries that already name the room

Try this in one minute

  1. Pick arrival and departure dates in the bar on the home page.
  2. On Rooms & Suites, show only Suites, or rooms for 4 or more guests.
  3. Open the Savanna Suite and tap Enquire on WhatsApp to see the message a guest would send.
