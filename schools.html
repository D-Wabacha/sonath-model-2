What keeps the school office phone ringing

Most of it is the same few questions, asked again every term.

  1. The same questions, every term

    Fees, opening dates, uniform, transport. Answered once on the website, they stop filling the office line.

  2. Admissions by word of mouth only

    New families search online first. If the school is not there, they find another one that is.

  3. Supporters cannot see how to help

    Sponsors and donors want to see the children's progress and a clear, trusted way to give.

  4. Notices lost in WhatsApp groups

    A news page gives every parent the same notice, in a place they can find again later.

Questions schools ask

Can the school update news and notices itself?

Yes. We add a simple page where a staff member can post a notice from a phone, with no technical knowledge needed.

What about photos of the children?

We help you choose photos that show the school without putting any child at risk, and we leave out names. Only use photos where parents have agreed.

Can parents pay fees on the website?

The website can show clear M-Pesa instructions at no extra cost. Matching each payment to a pupil automatically is part of a school system.

We are a small community school. Is this for us?

Yes. Wits Garden is a community school. Tell us what you can spend and we will tell you honestly what is possible for that amount.

Tell us about your school.

How many pupils, what parents ask most, and what you want the website to do. We reply within one working day.
