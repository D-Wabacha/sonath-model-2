Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Overview

This policy explains what information Sonath collects when you visit this website or get in touch with us, and how that information is used. Sonath is a small software development studio, and we collect the minimum information needed to respond to inquiries and run this website.

Information we collect

We collect information in two ways:

  • Directly from you, when you contact us by email, phone, WhatsApp, or the contact form on this site. This typically includes your name, email address, phone number if you provide one, and whatever you tell us about your project.
  • Automatically, through standard web server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited) generated by our hosting provider. We do not currently use analytics or tracking cookies on this site.

How the contact form works

The contact form on this site does not submit your information to a server or database that we control. Submitting it opens your device's email application with your message pre-filled, addressed to sonathsolutions@gmail.com. The information you enter is only transmitted once you send that email, using your own email provider.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your inquiry, discuss a potential project, and, if we proceed, deliver the work you've engaged us for. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies & third-party services

This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which may involve your browser making a request to Google's servers. We do not currently use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or analytics services on this site.

Data retention

We retain correspondence and project-related information for as long as reasonably necessary to maintain our business relationship and comply with any legal or accounting obligations, after which it is deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete it, by emailing sonathsolutions@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our practices change. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to sonathsolutions@gmail.com.
