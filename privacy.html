Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Overview

This policy explains what information Sonath collects when you visit this website or get in touch with us, and how that information is used. Sonath is a small software development studio, and we collect the minimum information needed to respond to enquiries and run this website.

Information we collect

We collect information in two ways:

  • Directly from you, when you contact us by email, phone, WhatsApp or the contact form on this site. This typically includes your name, email address, phone number if you provide one, and whatever you tell us about your project.
  • Automatically, through standard web server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages visited) generated by our hosting provider. We do not currently use analytics or tracking cookies on this site.

How the contact form works

When you submit the contact form, the details you enter are sent to our hosting provider, Netlify, which stores the submission and forwards it to us by email. Netlify processes this information on our behalf. We do not add you to a mailing list.

If the form cannot be sent, the page offers to open your own email application or WhatsApp instead. In that case nothing is transmitted until you choose to send the message yourself.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your enquiry, discuss a potential project and, if we proceed, deliver the work you have engaged us for. We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies and third-party services

This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which involves your browser making a request to Google's servers. We do not currently use tracking cookies, advertising pixels or analytics services on this site.

Data retention

We retain correspondence and project-related information for as long as reasonably necessary to maintain our business relationship and comply with any legal or accounting obligations, after which it is deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete it, by emailing sonathsolutions@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our practices change. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to sonathsolutions@gmail.com.
